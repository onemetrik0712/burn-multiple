Understanding Burn Multiple

Formula: Burn Multiple = Net Burn ÷ Net New ARR

The Burn Multiple measures capital efficiency by showing how many dollars you burn for every dollar of new ARR. Lower is better - it means you're growing efficiently.

Benchmarks:

  • < 1.0: Excellent - Highly capital efficient
  • 1.0 - 1.5: Good - Healthy growth
  • 1.5 - 2.0: Acceptable - Monitor closely
  • > 2.0: Concerning - Burning too much capital
